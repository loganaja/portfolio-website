THE OBJECTIVE

Overhaul existing robotic loading and unloading frames to reduce system complexity, lower unit costs, and enable modular adaptability across varying factory layouts. Lead frame, pneumatic, and electrical optimizations while improving the glass barrel dispensing mechanism and refining underlying robotic motion control software.

THE EXECUTION

Executed a design audit to remove redundant components, optimized the electrical and pneumatic Bill of Materials (BOM), and integrated structured cable routing and illumination. Redesigned the glass barrel dispenser by engineering a hopper adjustment mechanism, a low-friction Teflon gate, and a high-precision blade alignment assembly. Developed and refined Lua-based 3-axis motion control software, implementing homing routines, safety interlocks, adaptive unloading algorithms, and synchronized waiting functions to maximize duty cycle efficiency. Managed end-to-end component procurement and vendor logistics.

THE IMPACT

Delivered modular, universal frame architecture that eliminated recurring redesign cycles, increased robotic motion efficiency, and optimized dispensing reliability and system throughput.
