THE OBJECTIVE

During the final production stages of the Lapping 4 machine framework, I was tasked with implementing design enhancements to improve usability, prevent fluid leaks, and standardize components with the newer Lapping 5 model.

THE EXECUTION

I designed modified upper and lower machine doors to ensure cross-compatibility across both machine generations. To resolve operational fluid containment issues, I engineered improved, leak-proof drip trays. I managed procurement by generating a comprehensive Bill of Materials for the pneumatic systems and structural aluminum lighting fixtures, integrated aesthetic protective covers, and worked alongside senior engineers to install modules and program 3-axis robots for loading and unloading product from the machine while sending signals to the Lapping 5 PLC.

THE IMPACT

My enhancements significantly improved the machine's aesthetics and operational containment, while part standardization streamlined the assembly pipeline and ensured consistency across the manufacturing floor.
