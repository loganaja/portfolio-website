THE OBJECTIVE

The Lapping 5 Project required building a complete lapping machine from scratch, involving mechanical assembly, field wiring, and critical engineering optimizations to meet a strict shipment schedule. Working under the guidance of a senior engineer and electrician, the core tasks included identifying and correcting high-risk failure points in the system's indexing mechanism and rail, upgrading electrical routing, setting up pneumatic and sensor connections, and making necessary design modifications prior to shipping.

THE EXECUTION

All field cables and pneumatic airlines were routed and structured across six complex modules, utilizing electrical schematics to organize and label infrastructure for maintainability. To address mechanical failures with the original indexing rail, the rail was redesigned using aerospace-grade aluminum to eliminate thermal warping, span deflection, and crash damage. Additionally, fluid leaks were resolved, custom protective covers were fabricated, a 3D-printed reject tray was integrated, CAD updates were performed for the indexer motor assembly, and both the chipping robot vision camera and line automated inspection systems were programmed and optimized.

THE IMPACT

The Lapping 5 machine was commissioned and delivered on schedule. The redesigned index rails significantly reduced mechanical failure rates and were subsequently retrofitted to the Lapping 4 machine.
