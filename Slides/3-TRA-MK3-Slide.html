THE OBJECTIVE

I was tasked with producing 11 operational units of the Mk3 syringe cycler: 6 standalone portable testers and a 5-unit integrated test bench network. I was assigned a strict budget and timeline to scale the refined design into a final production-ready device.

THE EXECUTION

Serving as the project lead, I managed the entire lifecycle from generating a comprehensive Bill of Materials (BOM) to completing the mechanical CAD designs and assisting with the electrical cabinet layout. I selected and integrated a 10-inch touchscreen interface to eliminate external peripherals, iterated on the enclosure for noise reduction, and collaborated closely with senior engineers and electricians to bridge technical gaps in industrial compliance.

THE IMPACT

I successfully delivered all 11 production units, resulting in a compact, quiet, and highly maintainable fleet that standardized syringe durability testing across the facility.
